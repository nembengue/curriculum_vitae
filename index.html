About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at INRIA and Université Côte d’Azur. My research focuses on abstraction and analogical reasoning for improving knowledge representation in low-resource languages, with a particular emphasis on knowledge graphs and the Semantic Web.

More broadly, my interests lie at the intersection of Semantic Web, Knowledge Graphs, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Natural Language Processing (NLP). I enjoy working on approaches that combine symbolic and neural methods for reasoning and representation learning.
